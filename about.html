About

Hi, I'm Natasha.

I write for people who are tired, curious, and still hopeful.

I grew up in a small town in Michigan, the oldest of four, in a house where someone was always reading out loud. I started writing devotionals for my church newsletter in 2009. Those short pieces became my first book, and I haven't stopped since.

Today I live outside Chicago with my husband, two kids, and a garden that rarely goes to plan. When I'm not writing, I teach a Sunday class for young parents and speak at retreats and conferences.

Faith grows best in ordinary places.

My hope is that each book feels like a conversation with a friend over coffee. Nothing polished. Just honest words that help you take the next step.
