About

More Than My Résumé

I’m Natasha Logan, a mother, partner, author, entrepreneur, creator, traveler, cyclist, and lifelong believer in trying the thing that keeps calling your name.

My life has never followed just one lane.

I earned my Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Accounting from the University of Houston and built a career spanning more than 20 years in finance, developing a professional foundation that would eventually expand into operations, payroll, workforce scheduling, project support, and business systems.

But my professional career has always been only one part of who I am.

My family’s roots in Galveston, Texas, reach back to the 1800s. The Logan name entered our family through my great-great-great-grandfather during that time and became a name our family intentionally carried forward through generations. Rather than always following the traditional practice of taking our fathers’ surnames, the Logan name was preserved and passed down through our family line until it eventually reached me. Carrying it today connects me to generations of family history and to the place where our story in Galveston began.

Travel has become one of the great loves of my life. I’ve traveled to 22 countries, experiencing different cultures, places, food, people, and ways of life. Every trip has added something to the way I see the world and, in many ways, to the way I see myself.

Outside of work, I’ve followed my curiosity wherever it has taken me.

I’ve launched businesses, including A Travel Deal and Envi Me Athletic Wear. With Envi Me, I took an idea from product development and branding to photoshoots and a physical storefront. I later turned another idea into the Faith & Life Collection, writing and developing planners, a motivational book, and a companion workbook centered around faith, reflection, intention, and personal growth.

I love cycling and the freedom I find on two wheels. I bought a school bus and spent two years transforming it into my own Skoolie, doing nearly all of the work myself. I spent nights parked near the beach in Galveston under the stars, eventually bought land in Houston for it, and later sold it when life brought me to Chicago.

But beneath every career milestone, project, business, book, trip, and adventure is the part of my story that matters most: family.

I’m the mother of Envi and Germani, and I share my life with Chris. As we look toward the future, Chris and I hope to get married in Kenya and begin building a family of our own, adding another chapter to a life that has already taken me places I never could have completely planned.

I’ve learned that a meaningful life doesn’t have to fit neatly into one title.

You can spend more than 20 years building a career in finance and still discover entirely new parts of yourself. You can be a mother and still dream about what you want your own next chapter to look like. You can build something, let it go, begin again, travel somewhere unfamiliar, write the book, ride the bike, convert the bus, fall in love, and decide there is still more life to experience.

That is what this space represents.

Not simply what I’ve accomplished.

Who I’ve become along the way.

And I’m still becoming.
