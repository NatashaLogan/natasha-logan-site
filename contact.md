These days, I'm learning to appreciate what is happening right now while still leaving room for what's ahead.

And there is a lot ahead.

My daughter Envi is preparing to welcome a baby in October, which means I'm preparing for an entirely new chapter in my life: becoming a grandmother.

Chris and I are continuing to build our life together. Soon, we'll spend a few weeks in Kenya visiting his parents, creating new memories and continuing to bring our families and worlds together.

There are still places I want to see.

Ideas I want to explore.

Things I want to create.

People I want to love well.

And versions of myself I haven't met yet.

After more than 20 years in finance, raising my children, building businesses, writing books, cycling, transforming a school bus into a home, moving to a new city, traveling, falling in love and beginning again more than once, I've learned something important:

> There is no age when you have to stop becoming someone new.

I don't know exactly what the next chapter will look like.

I just know I'm not finished writing it.
