Envi Me Athletic Wear began as an idea I developed into a real brand. I worked through the product concept, sweat-wear design, branding, photography and presentation, eventually bringing the collection into a physical retail space. These photographs capture different parts of that process, from the garments themselves to campaign shoots, fittings and the storefront. For me, Envi Me represents creativity, entrepreneurship and the experience of building something from the ground up.
